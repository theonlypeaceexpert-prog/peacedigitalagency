## Interpretation and priorities

1. **Shopify SEO**: strong commercial fit, measurable demand in all five sampled markets and comparatively lower estimated difficulty. Use eCommerce SEO as the parent concept, not a duplicate thin page.
2. **Shopify design**: more direct hiring intent than generic website design. UK competition is notably stronger; focus on useful scope, mobile shopping, theme decisions and existing portfolio examples.
3. **Klaviyo**: smaller demand but highly relevant buying intent. US estimated CPC $18 and UK $39.31 indicate advertiser interest, not customer budgets or organic ranking difficulty.
4. **AI automation**: relevant service but mixed intent; many related questions are about starting an agency rather than hiring one. Avoid these founder-oriented questions and describe operational tasks, permissions and human oversight.
5. **UGC**: preserve and optimize the actual video portfolio; do not prioritize generic broad UGC words just because they have volume. US UGC video ads has KD61, so helpful creative examples and a clear brief matter.

Rejected: Klaviyo login, careers, misspellings, broad AI terms, repeated country keywords and generic 'best agency' claims. Popularity is not purchase intent. No search-history data was retrieved, so none of these is labelled a rising or trending term.

## Limits and next steps

- This covers five English-language market samples, not every country/language. Nigeria and other untested markets have unknown local demand. No automatic translation or fabricated local office claim was added.
- First-page rankings and orders cannot be promised. Indexing, useful differentiation, credible evidence, competition and legitimate third-party mentions matter alongside copy.
- Search Console domain verification and nine-page sitemap submission were completed in the preceding task. This task does not claim that new pages are already indexed or that the account's current performance was rechecked.
- Publish the updated app and deploy the same changes to the separate custom-domain host. Google cannot see editor-only changes; the sitemap grows from nine to thirteen pages only once the updated host serves it.
- After deployment, refresh Search Console and verify indexing for the four new URLs. Evaluate queries, impressions, clicks and real enquiries rather than celebrating keyword repetition. Compare results only after enough observations accumulate.
- Stronger case studies need the owner's verified role, client permission, dates and measurement sources. Do not reuse old demo-review statistics as verified external platform proof.
- Invite all past clients to honest reviews without incentives or screening for positive feedback. WhatsApp sharing is manual and does not automatically send messages or publish reviews.
